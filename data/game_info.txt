Valorant is a free-to-play first-person tactical hero shooter developed and published by Riot Games.
Players play as one of a set of Agents, characters designed based on several countries and cultures around the world.
In the main game mode, players are assigned to either the attacking or defending team with each team having five players.
Agents have unique abilities, each requiring charges, as well as a unique ultimate ability that requires charging through kills, deaths, orbs, or objectives.
Every player starts each round with a 'classic' pistol and one or more signature ability charges.
Weapons and ability charges can be purchased using an in-game economic system that awards money based on the outcome of the previous round.
VALORANT AGENTS AND ABILITIES

DUELISTS

Phoenix
Blaze: Makes a wall of fire that blocks vision and hurts enemies.
Hot Hands: Throws a fireball that hurts enemies.
Curveball: Throws a flash that blinds people who see it.
Run It Back (Ultimate): Marks a spot. Phoenix returns there if he is defeated or the timer ends.

Jett
Cloudburst: Throws a small cloud that blocks vision.
Updraft: Pushes Jett high into the air.
Tailwind: Lets Jett quickly dash in the direction she is moving.
Blade Storm (Ultimate): Gives Jett throwing knives that can deal heavy damage.

Raze
Boom Bot: Sends out a bot that looks for enemies and explodes near them.
Blast Pack: Throws a pack that can explode and push players away.
Paint Shells: Throws a grenade that breaks into smaller explosives.
Showstopper (Ultimate): Fires a powerful rocket.

Reyna
Leer: Sends out an eye that makes enemies who look at it see less clearly.
Devour: Uses an enemy’s soul orb to heal Reyna.
Dismiss: Uses a soul orb to make Reyna briefly untouchable.
Empress (Ultimate): Makes Reyna faster and improves her fighting ability.

Neon
Fast Lane: Creates two electric walls that block vision.
Relay Bolt: Throws a bolt that bounces and stuns nearby players.
High Gear: Lets Neon run quickly and slide.
Overdrive (Ultimate): Lets Neon fire a powerful beam of electricity.

Yoru
Fakeout: Sends out a copy of Yoru that can fool enemies.
Gatecrash: Sends out a teleport point that Yoru can travel to.
Blindside: Throws a flash that bounces off a hard surface.
Dimensional Drift (Ultimate): Lets Yoru move through another dimension for a short time.

Iso
Undercut: Fires a bolt that makes enemies easier to damage.
Double Tap: Gives Iso a shield after he shoots an energy orb.
Contingency: Sends a moving wall forward that blocks bullets.
Kill Contract (Ultimate): Pulls Iso and one enemy into a small arena to fight.

Waylay
Saturate: Throws light that slows nearby players.
Lightspeed: Lets Waylay dash forward.
Refract: Places a point Waylay can quickly return to.
Convergent Paths (Ultimate): Sends out a beam that slows enemies and gives Waylay a speed boost.

Hush
Flow: Places a point Hush can quickly move toward.
Flashbulb: Throws a flash that blinds players who see it.
Displacement: Throws an explosive that pulls and stuns nearby players.
Suspension (Ultimate): Sends out an ability that lifts nearby players into the air.

CONTROLLERS

Brimstone
Stim Beacon: Gives nearby teammates a boost to movement and weapon speed.
Incendiary: Fires a grenade that creates a damaging fire area.
Sky Smoke: Places smoke clouds that block vision.
Orbital Strike (Ultimate): Calls down a powerful attack on a chosen area.

Viper
Snake Bite: Creates a pool of damaging acid.
Poison Cloud: Creates a toxic smoke cloud.
Toxic Screen: Creates a long wall of toxic gas.
Viper’s Pit (Ultimate): Creates a large toxic cloud that hides and weakens enemies.

Omen
Shrouded Step: Teleports Omen a short distance.
Paranoia: Sends a shadow that makes enemies see less clearly.
Dark Cover: Places a smoke cloud that blocks vision.
From the Shadows (Ultimate): Lets Omen try to teleport anywhere on the map.

Astra
Gravity Well: Pulls players toward a point and makes them easier to damage.
Nova Pulse: Stuns players near a point.
Nebula: Creates a smoke cloud that blocks vision.
Cosmic Divide (Ultimate): Creates a huge wall that blocks bullets and sound.

Harbor
Storm Surge: Creates a water blast that slows and limits enemies’ vision.
High Tide: Creates a wall of water that blocks vision.
Cove: Creates a smoke that can also block bullets for a short time.
Reckoning (Ultimate): Sends a wave of water forward to slow and limit enemies’ vision.

Clove
Pick-me-up: Gives Clove a speed boost and temporary health after an enemy Clove hurt is defeated.
Meddle: Throws a blast that temporarily weakens enemies.
Ruse: Places smoke clouds that block vision. Clove can use it after being defeated.
Not Dead Yet (Ultimate): Lets Clove return after being defeated, but Clove must help defeat an enemy soon after.

Miks
M-pulse: Throws a device that can either heal teammates or stun players.
Harmonize: Gives Miks and a teammate a boost that refreshes when they get a kill.
Waveform: Places smoke clouds that block vision.
Bassquake (Ultimate): Sends out a sound wave that pushes back, slows, and deafens players.

Skye

Seekers (Ultimate): Sends creatures that chase nearby enemies.

KAY/O
FRAG/ment: Throws an explosive that damages enemies.
FLASH/drive: Throws a flash grenade.
ZERO/point: Throws a blade that stops enemies from using abilities.
NULL/cmd (Ultimate): Sends out pulses that stop enemies from using abilities.

Fade
Prowler: Sends a creature to chase and limit an enemy’s vision.
Seize: Traps enemies in an area.
Haunt: Reveals enemies it sees.
Nightfall (Ultimate): Sends out a wave that tracks, deafens, and weakens enemies.

Gekko
Mosh Pit: Throws a blob that explodes over a large area.

Dizzy: Sends a creature that flashes enemies it sees.
Thrash (Ultimate): Controls a creature that can leap at and hold enemies.

Tejo
Stealth Drone: Controls a drone that can reveal and stop enemies from using abilities.

Veto
